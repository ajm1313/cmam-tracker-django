WHO Chart Calibration Tool

This tool overlays computed Z-score lines (from LMS data) on top of the WHO chart images. Adjust the calibration sliders until the overlaid lines match the printed lines on the chart. You can also click on the chart to set precise reference points. Copy the output constants into your code when done.

Click-to-Calibrate

Method 1: Click "Calibrate X" then click 2 known X-axis tick marks on the chart.
Method 2: Click "Calibrate Y" then click 2 known Y-axis tick marks.
The tool will compute pxLeft/pxRight or pyTop/pyBottom from your clicks.
